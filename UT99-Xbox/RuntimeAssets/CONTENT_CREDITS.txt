Unreal Tournament Xbox 1.2 - Content Credits
=============================================

This port is intended for owners of Unreal Tournament. Unreal Tournament and
its original assets remain the property of Epic Games and their respective
rights holders. Community and third-party work remains the property of its
authors and rights holders.

The in-game credits cover the original Unreal Tournament team. This document
records the additional content distributed with the Xbox release. Original
readmes are retained in the release's Docs folder wherever they were available.


Console map pack
----------------

The included PlayStation 2 and Dreamcast map conversions preserve maps created
for the official console releases. Original game: Epic Games and Digital
Extremes. Refer to `Docs/Console_Map_Pack_README.txt` for the complete map list,
source platform, title, player count, and per-map author attribution.

The Phalanx and Sundial embedded world textures are recovered from the original
PS2 disc's retained mipmaps and palettes, with their original world scale.
EpicCustomModels.u is restored from the previously distributed RC1 System set;
this Epic Games package supplies the meshes imported by MultiMesh and
RocketArenaMultiMesh, including indirect PS2 character dependencies.

Authors represented in that map pack:

- Dave Ewing
- Eric "Ebolt" Boltjes
- Cedric "Inoxx" Fiorentino
- Juan Pancho "XceptOne" Eekels
- Rich "Akuma" Eastwood
- Alan "Talisman" Willard
- Warren Marshall
