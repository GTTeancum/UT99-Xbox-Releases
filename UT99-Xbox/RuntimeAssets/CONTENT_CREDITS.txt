Unreal Tournament Xbox - Additional Content Credits
===================================================

This Xbox build includes a small amount of community and third-party content in
addition to the original Unreal Tournament content. Credit remains with the
original authors and rights holders.

Maps and map dependencies
-------------------------

CTF-Titania
- Map author: Squacky
- Included dependency: UTDMT, by Patrick Cyr (GorGor)
- Included dependency: QUAKE3c texture package
- The Titania readme notes that most of the map textures are from Quake III.
  Original Quake III Arena artwork is credited to id Software.

DM-HangEmHigh
- Map author: jkcrmptn
- This map is an Unreal Tournament remake of Hang 'Em High from Halo: Combat
  Evolved multiplayer.
- The original Hang 'Em High map, Halo, and related Halo content are credited
  to Bungie and Microsoft.

DM-Halo-Derelict
- Map author: APOCALYPSE (Cirion / UT 2K4 / Perfect Chaos)
- This map is an Unreal Tournament remake of Halo "Derelict". The author's
  readme credits the Torlan UT2004 Derelict team for the earlier UT2004 version.
- Included dependencies: HaloTex texture package, sky texture package, 5-2SA1
  music package, and halochant music package.
- The author's readme credits Dwight Jack for the tracker tune "2 Souls at
  Once"; [^..^]Nick for suggesting secrets; Vlad's Army; and the Halo creators.
- Derelict, Halo, and related Halo content are credited to Bungie, Microsoft,
  and Gearbox.
